Profile

After 25 years in global banking product and transformation roles, I've spent the last couple of years going deeper into AI — not just advising on it, but building with it. I still lead the way I always have: starting with the underlying process and the user need, testing whether a solution is actually valuable and feasible, and staying close to delivery until it works in practice.

I help organisations get more value from technology, data and AI by identifying the right problems to solve, shaping practical products and programmes, and keeping business, technology and data science teams aligned through delivery and adoption. The case studies on this site — applied AI inside a regulated bank, an enterprise AI transformation programme, and a product I designed and shipped solo — are the practical evidence of that approach.
