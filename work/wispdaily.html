Product Build

WispDaily: a daily news podcast built for kids — and built to be safe

A fully automated daily children's podcast, designed, built and shipped to the App Store solo — turning the day's news and topics into something kids can actually listen to, with strict content and workflow guardrails built in from day one.

Role Founder & sole builder
Type Consumer AI productLive
Stack Claude Code, OpenAI, ElevenLabs
Audience Children & families — live on the App Store
Timeline May–Jun 2026, solo build

The idea

Kids' news content is either dumbed down, thin on substance, or simply doesn't exist in a format kids will sit through — while most "news" designed for kids is written once and read by a human on a fixed schedule, which doesn't scale to a daily habit. WispDaily set out to solve that: a genuinely daily, genuinely kid-appropriate news and topics podcast, generated and narrated with AI, so a fresh episode is ready every single day without a production team behind it.

The build

WispDaily is an end-to-end pipeline built solo, from architecture to App Store release: sourcing and selecting age-appropriate news and topics, rewriting them into scripts a child can follow and enjoy using OpenAI, turning those scripts into a listenable episode with ElevenLabs' high-quality voices, and orchestrating the whole daily build with Claude Code. Every part of that pipeline — selection, writing, narration, and publishing — had to be designed to run reliably without a human in the loop for each individual episode.

Safety & editorial safeguards

A product speaking directly into children's ears carries a different bar than a general-purpose AI app. The build treated safeguards as core product requirements, not an afterthought:

  • Source and topic filtering — screening what stories and subjects are eligible for a children's audience before anything is generated.
  • Age-appropriate rewriting — content is reframed in tone and language for a young audience, not simply summarised adult news.
  • Review checkpoints — generated scripts pass through review steps designed to catch unsafe, upsetting, or inappropriate content before narration and publishing.
  • Narration quality control — the audio experience is held to a consistent, high-quality bar so it's genuinely pleasant for a child to listen to daily.
Why this case study matters: WispDaily is the clearest proof of end-to-end AI build capability on this site — sourcing, generation, voice, safety, and daily automated delivery, designed, built and shipped solo to the App Store, not a prototype.
