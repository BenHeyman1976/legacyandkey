Hands-on AI Product Build

Building and shipping an automated AI audio product

WispDaily is a fully automated children's news and topics podcast: AI generates and edits the scripts, narrates them, and a new episode publishes every day. I designed, built and shipped it solo — from architecture to App Store release — with content safety and editorial controls built into the workflow from the start.

At a glance

Role Founder & sole builder
Organisation IndependentLive
Users Children & families — live on the App Store
Technology OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Claude Code

Problem

Most kids' news content is either dumbed down, thin on substance, or written once and read by a human on a fixed schedule — which doesn't scale to a daily habit. There was no genuinely daily, age-appropriate news and topics podcast that a small team, let alone one person, could sustain.

Why it was difficult

Publishing content daily with no human editor reviewing each episode raises the bar considerably for a product aimed at children. Every stage of the pipeline — sourcing, writing, narration and publishing — had to run reliably and safely without manual review of each individual episode, while still meeting a high bar for tone, accuracy and age-appropriateness.

Approach

I built WispDaily as an end-to-end pipeline: sourcing and selecting age-appropriate news and topics, rewriting them into scripts a child can follow using OpenAI, turning those scripts into narrated audio with ElevenLabs' voices, and orchestrating the daily build with Claude Code. Content safety was treated as a core product requirement rather than an afterthought, with source and topic filtering, age-appropriate rewriting, and review checkpoints designed to catch unsafe or inappropriate content before narration and publishing.

Outcome

WispDaily is live on the App Store. This write-up will be updated with usage data as it becomes available.

What this demonstrates

This is the clearest proof on this site that I don't just advise on AI — I understand implementation well enough to design and ship a working product: identifying a real user problem, orchestrating multiple AI services into a single pipeline, designing an editorial workflow and content-safety controls, automating daily production, and shipping to the App Store, solo.
